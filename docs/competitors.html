These are relevant alternatives and benchmarks, including products Rupert already connects to. The list is not the entire ERP market. No vendor performance or pricing has been independently benchmarked.

ERP suite

Odoo

Broad modular ERP; Community/Enterprise and paid app differences need a scoped quote.

Open-source ERP

ERPNext + Frappe HR

ERPNext core plus companion Frappe HR; module-specific deployment required.

Open-source ERP/CRM

Dolibarr

Modular self-hosted suite; connectors can require marketplace modules.

Enterprise ERP benchmark

Oracle NetSuite

Cloud ERP with core platform, optional modules and user licensing; implementation required.

Inventory suite

Zoho Inventory

Inventory plus Zoho Books/CRM and other integrated products; limits vary by plan.

Inventory operations

Cin7

Core and Omni differ; manufacturing/automation/integrations depend on product and plan.

Inventory operations

inFlow

Inventory, purchasing, barcodes and B2B; paid add-ons and limits vary.

Inventory / manufacturing

Katana

Commerce inventory and manufacturing; confirm selected manufacturing options.

Inventory / manufacturing

Fishbowl

Warehouse and manufacturing product family; verify edition and integrations.

Retail operations

Brightpearl

Retail automation, inventory planning, accounting and integrations.

Multichannel operations

Linnworks

Listings, channel integrations, routing and shipping; not a full HR/finance suite.

Retail POS

Lightspeed Retail

Retail POS, suppliers, analytics and eCom; product/region and plan matter.

Retail POS

Square for Retail

Retail workflows with separate Square Staff, Payroll, Loyalty and Marketing products.

Commerce / POS

Shopify POS

Commerce and POS, with app ecosystem extensions; POS Pro/plan restrictions apply.
