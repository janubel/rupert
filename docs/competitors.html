ERP suites, inventory specialists and retail POS platforms, compared against Rupert's actual implementation.

Directional capability comparison from the linked official product pages, not a hands-on vendor certification. Advertised means the cited product family explicitly describes the capability, subject to edition, region and plan. Integration / companion means a separate product, connector, or related module. Not established means the reviewed page does not establish the feature; it is not a claim of absence. Rupert Available means code exists, not independently verified live. This is a scoped set of relevant competitors, not an exhaustive inventory of the market. POS parity additions were reviewed against official vendor/manual/partner sources on 2026-10-15. Cell-specific sources and notes distinguish terminal time tracking from remote attendance, generic commission from the requested overlap formula, and offline checkout from offline inventory work. Unknown cells require vendor demonstration rather than implying absence.

Reading the matrix: Advertised = described by the linked vendor source · Integration / companion = separate product or connector · Not established = unknown from reviewed sources. Each vendor cell links to its supporting official page; there are no inferred negative scores.
