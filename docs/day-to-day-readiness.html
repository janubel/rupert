Inventory: next daily-use gates

1. Physically verify a small agreed sample. Select the actual C-Trax store and Shopify location, compare high-turn items and bulk/package variants with source records and shelf counts, and resolve discrepancies privately. Investigated/name-package candidates are suggestions; they withhold numerical differences until equivalence is established. An empty bounded exception queue is not a complete audit. 2. Use the right quantity for physical counts. New Shopify worksheets use native signed on_hand from the selected location snapshot, with catalog/shop/read-epoch identity checks. Missing, ambiguous, untracked, fractional or stale evidence must remain unavailable, not zero. Existing submitted comparisons keep their original basis and timestamps; legacy available/aggregate values do not become verified on-hand history. C-Trax worksheets retain their native store/variant comparison. Confirm the screen’s source, basis and observation time before interpreting a difference. 3. Pilot scanner and review discipline. Load a location sheet, use the exact stored SKU/barcode and enter the physically counted total. Prepared physical inputs already contain observed quantities, with negative observations starting at zero; adding scans without deliberately replacing/clearing the starting amount can overcount. Prove unknown/duplicate code handling and safe request retry. Missing-SKU Shopify rows and broader receiving/label/offline workflows remain separate work. 4. Define count correction/application. Approval freezes an audit; it does not change Shopify, Square or C-Trax stock. A later correction flow needs explicit authorization, an exact source item/location, a fresh before-value, concurrent-change handling, duplicate-safe recovery and post-write verification. Shopify/Square mutations and SKU edits require explicit owner signoff under AGENTS.md. 5. Accept real checkout stock effects separately. Paid Rupert sales can enqueue C-Trax stock removals when enabled. Validate the native removal/history contract and conduct explicitly approved controlled acceptance. A pending/unknown response must be investigated using its durable source marker, never blindly retried. Rupert allocations cannot reserve stock against independent C-Trax or Shopify sales. 6. Pilot bulk-material records. Establish verified opening lots and exact Shopify-variant gram recipes/effective dates, then reconcile a reviewed paid sale to its selected lot, movement and receipt. Current support is one material and one lot per whole sale line. Automatic sale consumption, edited/refunded-order corrections, split lots, multi-material recipes and Google Sheets synchronization remain unfinished. C-Trax retail sales do not automatically enter the Shopify-only material workflow.

Details: shared-stock comparison, C-Trax stock and counts, inventory workflows. Roadmap: F09–F13, F33–F35, F50 and F59. The on-hand worksheet change improves a local audit; it does not authorize source writes or establish physical stock accuracy.

Daily pilot and operations

- Morning: review source-specific successful acquisition and observation times in Credentials & sync, then stock comparison, ambiguous/stale findings, local holds and bulk balances. A configured schedule is not evidence of fresh vendor delivery. - During the day: continue the accepted checkout path; use Rupert’s reviewed counts, local holds and bulk receipts within their documented limits. Enable no new payment or stock-write setting just to test a screen. - Close: review native/local register controls, missing evidence and dated follow-ups; independently verify gateway and bank settlement. Keep counts, refunds and material corrections traceable. - Staff: assign minimum roles, link active workers and name count/time/close reviewers. Commission output remains a preview until basis, refunds, rounding and payout policy are approved.
