POS parity and acceptance

Implemented scope and the acceptance checks for the remaining work.

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Requirements come from the business's POS comparison supplied on 2026-10-12 and its associated planning conversation. This document contains a sanitized requirements summary; private conversations, attachments, contact details and account credentials are not published.

Implemented release scope

The application now includes an image-based Shopify catalog cart and server-priced quote with no-tip, percentage and custom tip choices. It does not complete a sale, calculate tax, collect a tip or charge a terminal. The existing POS remains the place to complete payments.

Remote clock-in/out is tied to an active worker record linked to the signed-in account. Workers request corrections from any authorized device; payroll reviewers approve or reject them with the original/requested timestamps retained. The 1% commission screen is a read-only estimate that gives each overlapping person the full percentage. It uses the imported order occurrence timestamp (some connectors use order creation time), a selected source/currency and a disclosed pre-discount item-price basis. It excludes incomplete payment coverage, recorded refunds and invalid item pricing. It does not finalize compensation or payouts.

Daily register summaries use separate order, payment and refund event dates, preserve source/location/currency groups, and export CSV. Tender/refund/net detail, missing/incomplete/excess/unknown payment coverage and source-specific import freshness are visible. Shopify tenders and their payment dates remain inferred; unsettled orders are excluded. Discounts, collected tips, merchant fees, C-Trax imports and terminal/bank reconciliation remain incomplete; unavailable values are not reported as zero. Gift/store-credit activity means tenders less refunds is not settled bank cash. The reviewed sales import guide documents the validated prepared SwipeSimple line-item CSV contract and its limits.

The role presets cover cashier/POS, inventory employee, reporting and admin. Store admins can assign store roles; super-admin access remains protected. New accounts without an explicit role default to cashier. Deactivated browser sessions are rejected. The API guide documents scoped, revocable read access; it is separate from payment-provider or terminal integration.

Provider account preparation, explicit KORONA/Cova read checks and selected Valmar Authorize.net/NMI credential/profile checks are documented in the connection guide. Read-access verification does not establish checkout readiness.

Primary POS cutover gate

Before replacing the current POS, demonstrate the selected merchant account/gateway and supported terminal, customer-facing tipping, taxes and discounts, failed/retried payments, refunds/tip reversals, loyalty and gift tenders, and daily source-to-bank reconciliation. The operator team must validate real checkout and register-close scenarios, retain a tested fallback, and approve cutover. A quote preview or vendor marketing page does not establish primary POS readiness.

Example overlap acceptance: worker A is present 09:00–12:00 and B 10:00–11:00. Two eligible sales of 100 currency units at 09:30 and 10:30 produce an estimate of 2 units for A and 1 for B. The overlapping sale gives each worker 1 unit; it does not split 1 unit between them. An order at clock-out belongs outside a half-open interval [clock_in, clock_out).

The first commission implementation must be labeled a preview. It must state its selected merchandise-only basis and source/currency filters, exclude unpaid or voided orders, and disclose unhandled refunds or adjustments. It must not finalize payroll, change approved timesheets or initiate payments. Approved time corrections must recompute the preview. Rules for the final payroll amount require a versioned, approved compensation policy.

Vendor evidence

KORONA documents terminal time tracking and general cashier/team commission configuration. Those do not establish remote employee punches or this exact overlap formula. Its manual documents tipping setup and register business-day closeouts. Time tracking, commission setup, tipping, end-of-day statements.

Cova advertises daily/closeout analytics, Cova Pay tipping and a KayaPush workforce integration. KayaPush describes mobile attendance; the exact per-person overlap commission and a specific daily per-register cash/card report require demonstration. Reporting, US tipping/payments, workforce companion, partner attendance.

C-Trax advertises tipping, retail analytics, access roles, multi-location operation and PAX device integration. Its exact shift/commission and tender report behavior require verification; the public page contains some incomplete integration descriptions. No particular terminal support cutoff is asserted here. Official product overview.

SwipeSimple documents tip settings and daily/batch reporting with cash/card details. Batch closing and daily report cutoffs vary by merchant provider; neither report alone proves a deposit reconciliation. Tip settings, daily and batch reports.

Valmar and Easyteam are integration candidates rather than ERP competitors. Valmar describes gateway/payment and reconciliation tools; merchant approval, supported products, gateway path and terminal compatibility remain account specific. Easyteam documents remote punches and generic commissions, while its default sales attribution is to a single staff member; the required overlap formula is not established. Valmar, Easyteam features, Easyteam attribution.

The business has an October replacement decision target. Vendor support dates, merchant account approvals and readiness for a particular processor must be verified directly; roadmap entries and marketing claims do not establish those facts.

Remaining implementation sequence

First preserve reliable sales inputs and register visibility: reviewed line-item CSV imports and source-aware coverage are implemented; C-Trax/native terminal and deposit ingestion remain pending a verified account contract. Next, preserve source line/variant identity for exact bulk gram recipes and reviewed local sale deductions, with shortage and replay audits. Automatic Google Sheets updates require a selected destination and reconciliation contract. Actual checkout, taxes/discounts, tip collection, supported terminal execution and payment/refund/settlement round trips remain the primary-POS cutover gate. Existing POS operation remains required until operator acceptance passes.
