# Square timecard history

Square history is read through `POST /v2/labor/timecards/search` with `TIMECARDS_READ`. No Square create, update or delete endpoint is used. The complete paginated read must finish before the tenant-serialized publication. A changed connection, duplicate source ID, pagination cycle or partial response prevents publication.

Run `TENANT_ID=... ACTOR_ID=... bundle exec rails square:timecards` to inspect matches without writes. Add `APPLY=1` to import locally. The actor must be an active account administrator with timesheet-write access in the tenant.

Automatic refresh runs on the server through Solid Queue every 15 minutes. It is disabled until an authorized administrator opts that tenant in with `TENANT_ID=... ACTOR_ID=... ENABLED=1 bundle exec rails square:timecards_schedule`; use `ENABLED=0` to disable. Only the tenant ID is queued; the encrypted tenant setting records the authorizing account and a configuration version. Each publication rechecks the active account, tenant, schedule version and Square connection. Revocation or disabling during a read prevents publication. A per-tenant job concurrency limit avoids overlapping scheduled reads, and a read older than an already-published refresh is rejected under the tenant writer lock. No employee account or HR setting is changed by scheduling.

Sync-attempt receipts record queued/running/succeeded/failed status without source payloads or credentials. Employee home and history show the last successful refresh and a safe failure notice. Failed/incomplete reads retain prior hours and the next scheduled run retries normally. Closed new shifts are counted once; open shifts wait until closed. Source corrections remain flagged for review with original imported hours retained.

Native timecard IDs are unique per tenant. Team-member IDs resolve only through retained Square employee source identities, including consolidated profiles. Names and email addresses are never used to guess identity. Inactive historical employees can receive history without activation, accounts or invitations.

Timecards and exact paid seconds per source-local date are retained in separate tables. Unpaid breaks are subtracted, paid breaks retained, and midnight/DST boundaries use the source IANA timezone. Zero-duration closed records remain visible with zero hours. Personal reports/calendar include each imported day once; source hourly wages provide basic estimates without changing employee pay-rate settings or inventing overtime, deductions, approvals or payment status. These historical records never enter new Rupert payroll generation.

Open, unmatched, invalid or overlapping records contribute no additional hours. Potential overlap with local manual daily hours is held conservatively; precise non-overlapping Rupert clock shifts may coexist. Local clock/entry writes share the importer’s tenant lock and reject overlapping imported history. Further employee consolidation moves source history and checks for overlap first.

A source correction, reassignment or disappearance retains the original imported hours and is flagged for review. Refreshes do not overwrite or delete historical days, infer payroll payment, or duplicate records. Original/latest source payloads are encrypted at rest and are not returned by the employee API.

Open **Timesheets → View Square history** or `/people/square_timesheets` for the full available retained Square history. This link opens without employee, date or status filters and does not inherit the Rupert submitted-sheet review queue. Pagination includes open, inactive-employee, unmatched and other held history. Authorized reviewers can narrow the view by employee, source status or original clock-in date; **Employee not matched** selects retained records without a consolidated employee.

Web history is limited to the signed-in worker for ordinary employees. Active reviewers need `timesheets.read` plus `timesheets.review` or `timesheets.write` to see all workspace employees; administrators, managers and shift leaders qualify through their default permissions. Active account administrators with timesheet-read access retain broader read-only history access without approval permission. Effective overrides apply, and inactive accounts/workspaces are denied. Historical employees do not need activation or invitations for a reviewer to inspect their records.

Detail pages show the latest observed source clock-in/out, timezone, source status and break start/end punches with paid/unpaid flags when returned. Open and changed records can therefore expose latest punches separately from original counted hours and unpaid break totals. **Download punches CSV** exports selected punch fields; a source punch change does not rewrite original counted days or establish Rupert approval or payment. Missing punches remain unknown. Pages and CSV do not expose the nested raw source payload.

The GET-only employee API at `/api/v1/employee/square_timecards` always forces its authenticated employee, including for native administrator sessions. CSV exports follow the same access boundary and escape spreadsheet formulas. Preview mode remains synthetic.
