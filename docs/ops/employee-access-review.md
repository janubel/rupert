# Employee access review

The employee directory shows an access badge and available workspace tabs for each linked login. Open **Review access** for tab-level View, Edit, Checkout or own-record capabilities. Disabled and unlinked accounts show no available tabs. Badges use the same effective permission resolution and navigation registry as the application, including individual and role overrides.

Account administrators can change the linked account's permission level and login status from the employee review. **Edit individual permissions** opens the existing per-person editor. Changes return to the same employee review and are recorded in the account activity log; HR fields and setup links are not regenerated. Only a super administrator may change a super administrator. Ordinary employees and HR-only managers cannot change account access.

The employee role retains the fixed POS and personal-time boundary. Per-person overrides cannot expand it into sales history, reconciliation, company reports or account administration. Changing a role is deliberate account administration: broader roles can expose company data, so the editor displays this distinction before saving. Simply viewing badges or opening the editor changes no roles or grants.
