# Business snapshot

**Money → Snapshot** (`/snapshot`) is a live, printable picture of the business, for the owner, an accountant, a lender or a new manager.

1. **Sales and where they come from.** Each source (Shopify, Square, C-Trax, SwipeSimple, Rupert POS) with a one-line description, sales over 30, 90 and 365 days, the last sale and the last successful import. Sources are listed side by side, never netted; a source with no recent sale is visible at a glance.
2. **Products and stock.** Active products, variants, Shopify↔Square links, stock value at price, low and out of stock, top sellers.
3. **Cash and money coming in.** Recorded tenders and refunds, the latest card-processor deposit report, bank-statement status (there is no live bank connection), the latest cash forecast and the last human approval of the books.
4. **Money out and vendors.** Expenses by category, vendor payments, what is owed, what is ordered but not received, per vendor.

A "Can I trust this today?" line at the top summarizes Books check and the approval ledger. Use **Print** (the navigation and buttons are hidden on paper). **Save a dated copy** retains an unchangeable JSON copy with a checksum as a generated original, so past snapshots can be compared later. Viewing needs finance read access; saving needs report-write access. Viewing and saving are written to the Activity log. It is an operational picture, not audited financial statements.
