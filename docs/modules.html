ERP modules

Every module registers in app/models/module_registry.rb, which drives the navigation and the permission gate. Features ship in seven areas.

Overview

Dashboard — a per-user customizable widget layout: key sales stats, per-channel revenue, attention items, stock alerts, and sync / reconcile / goal history. Widgets can be reordered and hidden.

Commerce

  • Sales — daily sales journal in spreadsheet style: an hourly × location pivot plus every sale of the day in arrival order, with a tracking indicator per order.
  • Registers — POS cash-drawer sessions: open a register, see sales per tender, and settle with a physical cash count at end of day.
  • Customers — unified CRM across channels with Ransack search, pagination, and lifetime value. Add, edit, and view each customer's order history.
  • Inventory — products and variants with independent per-location quantities, platform totals, and SKU linkage status.
  • Inventory counts — manual count sheets with a prepared-blank workflow, then submit / approve / reject / reopen.
  • Orders — detail page per order with a printable invoice and packing slip, shipping address, line items, payments, and fulfillment tracking.

Operations

  • Reports — sales, financial (P&L), inventory, and operations reports with 7/30/90/365-day windows and CSV export.
  • Reconcile — read-only SKU identity audit between Shopify and Square; independent quantities are never automatically equalized.
  • Ledger — the central transaction stream across both platforms, filterable by window and source.
  • Projects — projects and tasks with board, list, calendar and timeline views; task pages with checklist, comments, files, time, dependencies and repeating tasks; goals, team, templates, in-app notifications and CSV export. Projects start, hold, complete, archive and reopen.
  • Goals & KPIs — goals with activate / achieve / abandon, each with key performance indicators and reading history.

Purchasing

  • Vendors — supplier directory with payment terms and purchase-order history.
  • Purchase orders — draft → placed → received lifecycle, partial receiving, and what's owed to the vendor.

Finance

  • Chart of accounts — the account tree (assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expenses) with archive / restore.
  • Accounts — balance sheet and P&L overview built from the chart of accounts and sales.
  • Expenses — a money-out register with categories and soft delete.
  • Payments (AP) — vendor payments recorded against what's owed.

Team

The full People (HR) module set covers the staff lifecycle from directory to payroll:

  • Staff — HR records: contact details, department & position, employment type, and a status lifecycle (active / on leave / terminated / rehire) with emergency contacts.
  • Departments — the org chart: name, code, manager, and headcount per team.
  • Positions — job titles and pay grades, optionally tied to a department.
  • Timesheets — weekly hours per staff member with per-day entries (regular / overtime / PTO), moving draft → submitted → approved / rejected / reopened.
  • Leave & PTO — time-off requests (vacation / sick / personal / unpaid) with approve / deny / cancel and annual balance tracking.
  • Payroll — pay runs built from approved timesheets and current pay rates (hourly + salary), finalized and paid, with per-employee payslips (gross / deductions / net).
  • Accounts — login accounts (who can sign in) and role assignment.
  • Permissions — a role × permission matrix with save and reset-to-defaults, plus per-person overrides on each account.

System

  • Alerts — low-stock and attention flags that can be resolved or ignored per row.
  • Sync — run full, Shopify-only, or Square-only syncs; review run history and the log tail.
  • SwipeSimple import — import sales from a SwipeSimple CSV export (it has no public API) into the canonical sales stream, idempotently.
  • System health — server load, memory, swap, disk, database, and slow-query monitoring.
  • Connections — a plain-language guide to every service's keys: what's configured, where to find each key, and how to renew it.
  • Settings — env import/export, database backup/restore, Google Drive backup, and office feature flags.
  • Team chat — Slack-style rooms with threads, attachments, @mentions, reactions and the @rupert assistant (/chat). Rupert's automatic notices post to #announcements. Administrators only.
  • Warehouse — secret vendor links with price multipliers and bulk tiers, plus a public token-gated storefront for each vendor.
