# Employee timesheets and app access

Rupert serves the employee web app at `/employee`. Employees use the same account on the web, iPhone, and Android. The native client lives in `mobile/`; the installable web app is served by the Rails instance and needs no separate web hosting.

## Accounts and onboarding

1. An administrator creates an HR employee record with an email address.
2. On the employee page, **Create private setup / reset link** creates a least-privileged `employee` account and links that record. Existing non-employee accounts are managed through Accounts instead.
3. Share the displayed link privately with that employee. Rupert does not send email automatically. Links expire after 24 hours, work once, and are replaced when another link is issued. The cleartext link is never stored in the database.
4. The employee sets a password and signs in. The same email/password work in the native app, with the instance's HTTPS address.

Account administration and HR write access are both required to issue a link. Manager timesheet permissions do not grant account provisioning. Password setup/reset invalidates native sessions and employee browser sessions. An inactive account, inactive workspace, terminated employee, changed employee link, or ambiguous active worker mapping invalidates native access. Existing linked cashier/inventory accounts can use their own timesheets without receiving HR or payroll access.

Account email is globally unique in Rupert. Do not reuse a single login for several employees. Historical duplicate active employee links must be resolved by an administrator; the app refuses ambiguous worker mappings. New employee link assignments are validated for workspace membership and uniqueness.

## Timesheet workflow

- Employees create periods, add/edit/remove dated entries, enter notes, import closed clock shifts, and submit their own timesheets. Active reviewers need `timesheets.read` plus `timesheets.review` or `timesheets.write`. Administrators and managers qualify by default; the `shift_leader` role grants review without payroll or account administration. Existing permission overrides continue to apply.
- Entries support regular, overtime, PTO, and holiday hours. Dates must fall within the period and cannot be in the future. Periods cannot overlap for the same employee and are limited to 31 days. All entries for a worker/day together must be at most 24 hours.
- Submitted/approved/rejected entries cannot be edited. A reviewer cannot approve or reject a record linked to their own login. Review decisions and comments are atomic and audited. Invalid/repeated state changes cannot silently replace an earlier review. Submission and approval check active employment, valid periods and entries, daily totals, current imported clock hours and pending clock corrections. A reviewer can still return an invalid sheet for correction.
- A rejected sheet can be reopened by its employee. An approved sheet requires a manager to reopen it. A sheet included in a payslip cannot be reopened until that payroll snapshot is removed/regenerated by an authorized payroll operator. Records with submission history cannot be deleted through the timesheet workflow.
- Clock imports split overnight shifts at midnight in the **instance time zone** (currently Eastern Time), including daylight saving boundaries. Imports rebuild only the rows attributed to clock shifts, preserve manual rows, and reject pending corrections. Re-importing does not add duplicate hours. Submitting checks for stale imported clock times and requires a fresh import if necessary.
- Imports classify hours as regular. Review manual entries to avoid counting the same shift twice and classify overtime according to the organization's rules. Overtime thresholds and pay rules are not inferred from the employee's device locale.
- An approved clock correction does not rewrite a submitted/approved timesheet or payslip. Reopen and re-import when permitted, or handle the adjustment through payroll.
- Single-sheet and filtered-list CSV exports respect the same access boundaries and neutralize spreadsheet formulas in text fields. Regular payroll generation includes only approved **entry dates inside the pay run**, rather than all hours on an overlapping sheet.

The named **Timesheets** work area opens the submitted **Needs review** queue for permitted reviewers, with separate All timesheets, Approved and Returned for correction filters. The pending count covers visible submitted sheets regardless of the selected filter. Open **Review timesheet** to inspect daily hours, overtime and notes against shifts; the on-page checklist explains the checks before approval. Employees continue to see only their own records and need another reviewer for their hours. Assign review duties explicitly; this release does not change existing staff roles.

**Timesheets** also links directly to all Rupert timesheets, Square history and C-Trax history. The source links open the full available retained history without inheriting the submitted-sheet queue or applying date, employee or status filters. Authorized reviewers and account administrators with timesheet-read access can filter all employees, including inactive and unmatched history; ordinary employees retain their own-record boundary. Source records include open and held records, with C-Trax records lacking a store assignment held separately as unassigned. Square details expose latest clock and break punches alongside original counted hours. C-Trax details distinguish latest and original clock/hour evidence and retain an encrypted, immutable observation sequence for corrections, reversals and disappearance/reappearance. Missing C-Trax breaks are not inferred. CSV exports follow the same access boundary and neutralize formulas.

These source views do not approve hours or generate payroll. Counted Square history retains its existing personal-report/calendar treatment; C-Trax history does not enter personal totals or payroll. Do not add overlapping source hours together. See the [employee source-history guide](ops/employee-source-history.md) for access, punches, identity and recovery details. The expanded source-history implementation was deployed and complete source reads succeeded on October 17, 2026; authenticated access/render and pre/post-deployment recovery checks passed; see the [source-history deployment receipt](ops/employee-source-history-deployment-2026-10-17.md).

Automatic C-Trax labor refresh is enabled and verified on SER8 for Herbal Healers as of October 17, 2026. An active account administrator with account-management and timesheet-write permission can opt the workspace in from C-Trax history. Server reads dispatch every 15 minutes at minutes 02, 17, 32 and 47; queue delays and complete-source validation affect actual pickup time. Reviewer status visibility does not grant configuration permission. See the [automatic refresh workflow and limits](ops/ctrax-timesheet-refresh.md).

The workflow uses a transaction and tenant writer lock before overlap checks, daily totals, mutations, and reviews; this supports SQLite and PostgreSQL. Payroll generation takes the same lock before reading approved hours. Timesheet events preserve changes, actor, and review history. The existing activity trail records creation/deletion and workflow actions.

## Web app installation and device sessions

Visit `https://YOUR_INSTANCE/employee`:

- iPhone/iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App.
- Android: Chrome → Install app/Add to Home screen.
- Desktop: use the browser's install action when supported.

The instance serves its manifest, icons, installation page, privacy/support page, and service worker. HTTPS is required for production installation. The service worker does **not** cache employee records or credentials. There is no offline queue: a clock/time entry is saved only when the server confirms it. The offline screen says explicitly that no hours were saved by that page.

Employees can revoke their own device sessions at `/employee/devices`. Native session tokens expire after 30 days and are stored as digests on the server; the app stores the token in Expo SecureStore with device-only accessibility. The native app never stores the password. Web cookies cannot authorize the employee API, and employee app tokens cannot authorize Rupert's catalog/admin API.

The employee workspace contains no installation or store promotion. Distribute native apps separately when the owner is ready; signing and store submission remain deferred.

Administrators can configure actual effective-dated pay rates and annual leave allowances from the employee's HR page, under payroll-write or leave-write permission respectively. New rates preserve earlier history; future rates do not become current before their effective date. Allowance updates preserve used hours. No real rates or allowances are filled automatically.

## Personal workspace and reports

Employee password and Google sign-ins land at `/employee`: a simple home with clock status, this week's hours, estimated gross pay and a shortcut to their current timesheet. Opening the week reuses an existing overlapping period and forces ownership to the signed-in worker. Native device management is a separate secondary page. Administrators without a linked employee can inspect a clearly marked, read-only example with `?preview=1`; no accounts, time records or rates are created by that preview.

`/employee/time-off` shows only the signed-in worker's recorded hours and requests on a monthly calendar. Empty days do not imply scheduled days off. Employees request vacation, sick, personal or unpaid leave, track review status and cancel unreviewed requests. Reviewed requests require a manager to change them. Paid requests require the employee's requested working hours; requests crossing years must be submitted separately for each year. Recorded allowances are shown; missing allowances are not invented. Manager approval/cancellation uses a serialized transaction so retries cannot consume or restore hours twice, and cancelling an unapproved request never changes used hours.

`/employee/reports` provides a personal date range, hour and time-off CSV downloads, estimated gross pay and only that employee's finalized/paid payslips. Dates are validated and limited to one year. Estimates use the hourly rate effective on each entry date and the existing multiplier for explicitly marked overtime. They include draft entries, exclude unimported clock shifts/commissions/deductions and are not payment instructions or promises. Missing rates and salaried periods have no hourly estimate; finalized payslips retain their recorded deductions, net pay and payment status/date. No future payment date is inferred. Separate clock totals avoid counting imported shifts twice.

Employees on leave retain personal read access and time-off requests but cannot clock in while their worker record is on leave. Terminated or ambiguous links do not authorize a personal workspace. Employees can also save private time/pay/time-off problem reports at `/employee/reports`. Reports retain the original request, track open/in-review/resolved status and show the latest administrator response. Submission keys make retries safe without duplicate reports. Only active account administrators with HR read/write access can review/respond at `/people/work_reports`; a reviewer must reload stale forms before overwriting another response. The intake sends no email, external message or background job. Reports follow canonical employee profiles during consolidation, and deleting an employee with report history is blocked. Clock corrections remain available from the employee's own time-clock history and require review. All personal pages and exports are `no-store`, sensitive form parameters are filtered from request logs, and cross-worker/tenant identifiers never select the personal worker.

## API contract (v1)

Base: `/api/v1/employee`. Requests/responses are JSON. Except login, supply `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. All responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`.

| Method and path | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /session` | `{email,password,device_name}` → `201 {token,expires_at,employee,workspace}`. Shares the browser password throttle; returns 429 when blocked. |
| `GET /session` | Current employee/workspace and session expiry. |
| `DELETE /session` | Revoke this device; 204. |
| `GET /timesheets` | Own recent timesheets (up to 104). |
| `POST /timesheets` | `{timesheet:{period_start,period_end,notes}}`. Employee identity is forced server-side. |
| `GET /timesheets/:id` | Own sheet, entries, review comment, and history. |
| `PATCH /timesheets/:id` | Update a draft period/notes. |
| `DELETE /timesheets/:id` | Delete a draft without submission history. |
| `POST /timesheets/:id/entries` | `{timesheet_entry:{worked_on,hours,work_type,notes}}`. |
| `PATCH /timesheets/:id/entries/:entry_id` | Edit an entry on this draft. |
| `DELETE /timesheets/:id/entries/:entry_id` | Remove an entry on this draft. |
| `POST /timesheets/:id/actions/submit` | Submit non-empty draft. |
| `POST /timesheets/:id/actions/reopen` | Reopen own rejected sheet; native API never reopens approved records. |
| `POST /timesheets/:id/actions/import_shifts` | Rebuild clock-attributed draft entries from closed shifts. |
| `GET /clock` | Own recent shifts (up to 100). |
| `POST /clock` | `{request_token}` (16–64 URL-safe characters). Retry the same clock-in token safely. |
| `PATCH /clock/:id` | Clock out own shift; safe to retry. |
| `POST /clock/:id/corrections` | `{clock_correction:{proposed_started_at,proposed_ended_at,reason}}`. Times are ISO 8601 with offset; reviewer approval is still required. |

IDs belonging to another worker or workspace return 404. Missing, revoked, expired, or inactive credentials return 401. Unsupported review actions/invalid entries return 422; role denial returns 403. Native APIs never accept employee IDs, review decisions, token scopes, or administrator role changes from clients. Browser endpoints remain CSRF protected.

### Consolidated employee directory

Administrators with HR write and account management permission can use **Employees → Consolidate profiles** after confirming two app accounts belong to the same person. Choose the primary name and contact details and record the identity evidence. Source identities are stored separately from the canonical employee; directory refreshes update those snapshots without overwriting curated HR details or creating the merged profiles again. Source filters include every linked app.

Original employee rows remain private audit records with a canonical pointer. Old employee URLs redirect to the primary profile. Consolidation moves timesheets, entries through their parent timesheets, leave, pay history, department manager assignments, clock shifts and retained Square/C-Trax history; it revokes outstanding setup links and app sessions. Conflicting linked accounts, HR details, overlapping counted time records, duplicate leave balances or duplicate payslips stop the transaction for review. Pay rates on more than one profile also require review before consolidation. C-Trax observations remain separate review evidence without adding counted hours. Employees with source history cannot be deleted through the normal workflow. No upstream app data is changed.

Private setup links last 24 hours, work once, and replace any previous unconsumed link for that employee. Shared employee emails, explicitly inactive source accounts and existing unlinked accounts require review before provisioning. Existing privileged accounts retain their roles and use their existing login; employee setup links cannot reset administrator accounts. Generate links only for confirmed current staff, and share each link directly with its intended employee. Link creation does not send email. Keep link exports private and outside Git. A source that does not supply activity status requires an employment decision by the administrator.

## Employee-only authorization boundary

The `employee` role is fixed to `pos.use` and `timesheets.self`, including when a legacy person or role override contains a wider permission or wildcard. Account and permission editors cannot expand it. To approve someone for broader duties, an administrator must explicitly assign a different role; this is separate from inviting them for employee time entry. Employee browser sessions are limited to the permitted POS/customer-display routes and their own time workspace; they cannot access sales history, reconciliation, company finance/payroll workspaces, customer directories or administrative API credentials. Native bearer credentials cannot access POS or other business/admin APIs. Native credentials only authorize the employee API and that worker's own records.

Deactivating an account denies its existing browser and API sessions on the next request. Preserve employee identities, time history, existing setup links and held profiles when changing access. Audit actual financial routes as well as the navigation, and rehearse authentication with synthetic passwords only on a private database copy.
