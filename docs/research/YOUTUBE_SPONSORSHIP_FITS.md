# YouTube Sponsorship Fits — Herbal Healers Products

Research note for sponsoring herbal/wellness product sponsorships. No live
YouTube data pulled — these are starting points to vet before outreach.

## Fit categories
- Herbalism & natural-remedy educators
- Functional wellness / supplement & CBD reviewers
- Holistic health / mindful living
- Homesteading & DIY herbalists

## Specific creators to vet
- The Homegrown Herbalist
- LearningHerbs (and Rosemary Gladstar's circle / "Herbs with Rosalee" — Rosalee de la Forêt)
- Green Witch–style herbal channels
- CBD / supplement reviewers with an earthy wellness angle

## Vetting checklist before pitching
- Subscriber count vs. typical views (vlog style overstates reach)
- Audience age/skew and fit with our products
- Whether they already review competing products
- Tone aligned with a compliance-safe wellness brand
- Engagement rate and comment authenticity
